## What Surprised Me Most

### 1. The Freedom We Were Given
- Being allowed (and encouraged) to choose what to build for React and Django exercises.
- How this freedom made learning more enjoyable and ownership-driven.
- Contrast with typical "follow exact instructions" expectations you might have had.

### 2. Having a Co-intern Who’s Also a Friend
- How having a friend as co-intern made communication natural and easy.
- Mutual help, bouncing ideas, and emotional support.
- The advantage of not starting from zero in terms of relationship-building.

### 3. The Mentoring & Collaboration Culture
- First real collaborative experience in a long time after years of freelance.
- Daily interactions with mentors/senior devs.
- Learning through discussions instead of just self-research.

## The Turning Point: My Setback Moment

- The technical mistake: Relying on the wrong technology → had to rewrite a big part.
- Initial fear: Expecting punishment or harsh feedback (based on freelance experience).
- What actually happened:
  - Team gave an extension.
  - Supportive check-ins ("What do you need?").
  - Constructive feedback instead of blame.
- Why this was eye-opening: Huge contrast with rigid freelance client work.
- Lesson: Psychological safety accelerates learning.
